# 从讲述到推理链：倪海厦紫微斗数断法的逐步推理引擎

——以倪海厦《天纪》讲稿为唯一规则来源

## 摘要

倪海厦看命盘是一步接一步的：先看出这个人属于哪一类，再由个性推到行为，最后推到成败。本文把他《天纪》讲稿里「后一个判断由前一个判断推出」的推导逐条抽出来，做成推理链知识库（1685 步、1028 个判断结点，每一步附原话）和第四版引擎。抽取、核查、归一都由相互独立的 AI 实例完成。

事先登记的主检验问：剔除倪海厦讲某位命主那几分钟里的全部步之后，本人的命盘是否比随机盘更容易推出他对这个人的判断？24 人的结果是单侧 p = 0.066，没有达到事先定的 p < 0.05，未能证明引擎能复现他对命例的具体判断。

此后的补充研究都在运行前登记：
- **认本人**：给 AI 审者倪海厦对某人的描述和四份先天论断，挑出由本人命盘推出的一份。第二次检验的题目互相独立，每题统一 6 条陈述，陪衬是每题独用的随机盘。审者挑中本人的比例为 0.56（47/84；瞎猜 0.25；p < 0.001），比陈述换成别人时高 0.24（p = 0.015）；但审者也可能偏好真人盘本身。
- **推理方式**：借用《易传》解释卦爻的体例的名目，给全部起步、推理步与《紫微斗数全书》725 条断语归类（一致程度 α 为 0.83–0.87）。倪海厦由盘面起步的判断与《全书》断语结构相近，多凭位置或直断，但明说借象（10.9% 对 1.5%）与明说道理（8.4% 对 0.5%）多得多；推理步则多凭人情事理（39%）与类别（23%）。
- **召回率**：只算明确漏掉的，推理步约九成抽到了（至少 0.83）。

推理链把倪海厦明说的推导写成了可运行、可逐步核对的程序：讲出来的推导很短，起步承接古典断语的体例，往后多凭人情事理；引擎推出的论断整体上贴近他对一个人的描述，却推不回他说过的那几个具体判断。

**关键词**：紫微斗数；倪海厦；推理链；前向推理；易传；数字人文

**英文题目**：From Lectures to Reasoning Chains: A Step-by-Step Inference Engine for Ni Haixia's Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart Reading

**Abstract**: From Ni Haixia's *Tian Ji* lectures alone, we extracted every stated step in which one judgment about a person is derived from another, building a chained knowledge base of 1,685 steps (1,028 judgment nodes, each tied to Ni's words) and a forward-chaining engine; extraction and checking were done by independent large-language-model instances. The preregistered main test, whether a person's own chart reproduces Ni's case judgments better than random charts once the relevant minutes are removed, was not passed (24 persons, one-sided p = 0.066). In preregistered follow-up studies, AI judges picked the reading derived from the person's own chart among four natal readings in 47 of 84 judgments (0.56; chance 0.25; p < 0.001) in a redesigned test with independent items, six statements per item and single-use random-chart foils, and more often than when the description belonged to someone else (difference 0.24, p = 0.015), although judges may also favour real charts as such. Coding all start and inference steps and 725 judgments from the classical *Zi Wei Dou Shu Quanshu* with categories borrowed from the *Yi* commentaries' interpretive modes (α 0.83–0.87) showed that Ni's chart-to-judgment steps resemble the classical text, resting mostly on positional relations or bare assertion, but give images (10.9% vs 1.5%) and reasons (8.4% vs 0.5%) far more often, whereas his inference steps rest mostly on human affairs (39%) and categories (23%). About nine in ten stated inference steps were extracted (lower bound 0.83). The engine's readings fit Ni's description of a person as a whole, yet do not recover the specific judgments he made.

**Keywords**: Zi Wei Dou Shu; Ni Haixia; reasoning chain; forward chaining; *Yijing* commentaries; digital humanities
